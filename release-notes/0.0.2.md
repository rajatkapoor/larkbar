### New
- **Voice levels.** Live level bars in each mic's row show your voice coming in. Click
  **Show Voice Levels** to turn them on; macOS asks for microphone permission once. LarkBar only
  measures loudness while the menu is open and never records or sends audio.
  In Stereo every mic gets its own bar; in Mono the receiver mixes the mics, so several mics on
  share one combined bar.
- **Menu bar styles.** Choose what the menu bar shows: just the icon, each mic's battery
  percentage, or a small coloured battery bar per mic. The choices preview the real icon.
- **Clearer mute button** at the start of each mic's row. It turns red when the mic is muted.

### Changed
- The Sound section is now labelled "Sound (all mics)", since those settings are shared by every
  mic on the receiver.

### Install
Download `LarkBar-0.0.2.dmg`, open it, and drag LarkBar to Applications (replace the old copy if
asked). Requires macOS 14 or later. Signed and notarized by Apple.
